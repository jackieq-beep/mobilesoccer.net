Why in-game sponsorship works.

Brands perform best inside the flow of play, across repeat sessions, in a setting where sponsors already belong.

Players are paying attention

Mid-match, players are focused on the screen. Your brand is seen with the same attention as the game itself, not scrolled past.

They see it again and again

Leagues, live events and daily challenges bring players back to the stadium many times a week. Every visit is another exposure.

It feels natural

Football has always had sponsors on the boards, the kits and the screens. Placements fit what players expect, so they don't feel like an interruption.

How a campaign runs.

You work directly with the studio, from first brief to final report.

  1. Brief

    Tell us your markets, dates, goals and the placements you're interested in.

  2. Plan

    We recommend formats, regions, timing and share of voice, including exclusivity options.

  3. Go live

    We build your creative into the game, you approve it, and it goes live on schedule.

  4. Report

    You get results by placement and region during and after the campaign.

Measurement that fits gameplay

Impressions are only the start. Reporting is built around how players actually see your brand during matches.

  • Impressions by placement and region
  • Time on screen during live match moments
  • A post-campaign report for your brand and agency
