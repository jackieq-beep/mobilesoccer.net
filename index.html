Meet Mobile Soccer Media: brand partnerships inside Football League

The studio behind Football League is opening its matches to brands, with sponsor placements built into the game on the pitch, in the stadium and around the moments players care about most.

Mobile Soccer Media is the new partnerships arm of Mobile Soccer, the independent studio that makes Football League. It gives advertisers and agencies a direct way to put a brand inside a free mobile soccer game that players around the world return to every day.

Instead of interrupting play, sponsors appear where football fans expect to see them: on the boards around the pitch, on the big screens, and in the presentation of every match.

A global audience that keeps coming back

Football League is free on iOS and Android and is played across Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa and the Middle East. Leagues, live events and daily challenges keep players returning, which means a brand placed in the game is seen across many sessions, not just once.

Part of the match, not an interruption

Because we make the game, we can design sponsor placements into it from the start. Brands can appear on pitch-side LED boards, stadium screens and roof banners, in Player of the Match moments, and across branded events and rewards.

Campaigns can run worldwide or in the regions you choose, with frequency control, and we fine-tune placements while the campaign is live.

Football has always had sponsors on the boards and the shirts. Our aim is simple: make a brand feel like it belongs on matchday, so players notice it without ever feeling interrupted.

The Mobile Soccer Media team

What partners can do

  • Match Takeovers. One brand owns a complete in-game event: its matches, rewards and the screens around it. It's the closest thing to sponsoring a tournament.
  • In-game integrations. Branded moments inside the match, such as Player of the Match, plus branded rewards and items, all built around your creative.
  • Always-on stadium inventory. Pitch-side LED boards, big screens and roof banners that build familiarity match after match.
  • Targeting and reporting. Choose your regions and frequency, then track impressions by placement and region, time on screen in live matches, and a full end-of-campaign report.

What a partnership could look like

  • A sportswear launchTakes over a weekend cup event, with branded matches and an exclusive in-game reward for players who complete it.
  • A drinks brandRuns always-on pitch-side boards in selected markets for a full season, with frequency control and monthly reporting.
  • A telecom or tech partnerPresents Player of the Match in every game for the length of a campaign, alongside stadium screen placements.

These are illustrative examples. Every partnership is planned around the brand's own goals.

Common questions

What is in-game advertising?

In-game advertising places a brand inside the game itself rather than in a pop-up or video break. In Football League that means pitch-side LED boards, stadium screens, Player of the Match moments and branded events, the same places sponsors show up in real football.

Which game are the placements in?

Football League, a free soccer game for iOS and Android with over 100 million downloads, made by our studio, Mobile Soccer.

Can we target specific countries?

Yes. Placements can run worldwide or only in the regions you choose, with frequency control.

Is it brand safe?

Yes. You work directly with the studio, with no open ad networks. You approve every creative, placement rules are agreed upfront, and your brand only appears in a football setting.

How do you report results?

Impressions by placement and region, time on screen during live match moments, and a full report when the campaign ends.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the format, markets, dates and exclusivity. Send us a brief and we'll reply with available inventory and a plan that fits your budget.
