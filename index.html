Why in-game sponsorship works.

Brands perform best inside the flow of play, across repeat sessions, in a setting where sponsors already belong.

Players are paying attention

Mid-match, players are focused on the screen. Your brand is seen with the same attention as the game itself, not scrolled past.

They see it again and again

Leagues, live events and daily challenges bring players back to the stadium many times a week. Every visit is another exposure.

It feels natural

Football has always had sponsors on the boards, the kits and the screens. Placements fit what players expect, so they don't feel like an interruption.

Questions advertisers ask.

What is in-game advertising?

In-game advertising places a brand inside the game itself, not in a pop-up or video break. In Football League, that means your brand on pitch-side LED boards, scoreboards, Player of the Match screens and branded events, the same places sponsors appear in real football.

Which game are the placements in?

Football League, a free mobile soccer game for iOS and Android with over 100 million downloads, made by our studio, Mobile Soccer. Manchester City is officially licensed in the game.

Which markets can we reach?

The audience is global, and placements can be targeted by region, so a campaign can run worldwide or in selected markets only.

Is it brand safe?

Yes. You buy directly from the game studio, with no open ad networks. You approve the creative, placement rules are agreed upfront, and your brand only appears in football settings.

How are results measured?

You get impressions by placement and region, time on screen during live match moments, and a post-campaign report for your brand and agency.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the format, markets, dates and level of exclusivity. Send us your brief and we'll come back with available inventory and a plan for your budget.
